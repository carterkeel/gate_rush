You've seen the ads. Run through the gates, grow your army, crush the enemy crowd, and win. Then you install the game and it's something else entirely.

Gate Rush: Multiply & Conquer is the game from those ads, and you actually get to play it.

FREE. OPEN SOURCE. NO ADS.
No ads, no pop-ups, no timers, no accounts and no tracking. The whole game is free, and the full source code is open for anyone to read at github.com/carterkeel/gate_rush.

HOW TO PLAY
Slide to steer your army down the track. Pick the gates that grow your crowd and dodge the ones that shrink it. At the end of every stretch the enemy is waiting, so the bigger your army, the more of them you crush. Reach the boss with enough soldiers and it falls.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Simple one-thumb controls and a short tutorial
• Gates that add, multiply, subtract and divide
• Big crowd-versus-crowd fights and boss battles
• Instakill and Double Army power-ups
• Gold to spend on upgrades
• Skins and gate trails to customize your army
• Top scores to chase your best run

Your progress is saved on your device only, and we don't collect your data. Found a bug? Use Report a Bug in Settings.

Download it and enjoy the game the ads promised.
