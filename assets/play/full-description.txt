You've seen the ads, run through the gates, grow your army, crush the enemy crowd and win, then you install the game and it's something else entirely - Gate Rush: Multiply & Conquer is the game from those ads and you actually get to play it - it's completely free, open source and has no ads, no pop-ups, no timers, no accounts and no tracking, and the full source code is public on GitHub for anyone to read - slide to steer your army down the track, pick the gates that grow your crowd and dodge the ones that shrink it, because at the end of every stretch the enemy is waiting and the bigger your army the more of them you crush, reach the boss with enough soldiers and it falls - you get simple one-thumb controls, a short tutorial, gates that add, multiply, subtract and divide, big crowd-versus-crowd fights, boss battles, Instakill and Double Army power-ups, gold to spend on upgrades, skins and gate trails to customize your army and top scores to chase your best run - your progress stays on your device and we don't collect your data, and if you find a bug just use Report a Bug in Settings - download it and enjoy the game the ads promised
